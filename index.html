About me

Operations experience, translated into better software.

My background comes from national courier and logistics operations across first-mile, middle-mile, last-mile, returns, performance reporting, and distributed team leadership.

That experience taught me where operational systems usually fail: unclear ownership, fragmented information, manual handoffs, weak exception visibility, and tools designed without the operator in mind.

Today I build from both sides of the problem. I understand the operational pressure, then use backend architecture, data modelling, APIs, automation, and thoughtful interfaces to create systems people can genuinely use.

“I do not just want to build software for operators. I want to build software that operators would genuinely choose to use.”

Engineering principles

Calm systems. Clear boundaries. Reviewable progress.

  1. 01
    Operational clarity over decorative complexity

    The interface should help someone decide and act.

  2. 02
    Secure defaults over temporary shortcuts

    Trust boundaries belong in the foundation, not the backlog.

  3. 03
    Small, reviewable changes over uncontrolled rewrites

    Good delivery stays understandable, testable, and reversible.

  4. 04
    Honest prototypes over fictional production claims

    Demonstrations should impress without pretending.
